Changelog

New features and fixes in bimp, newest first.

14 November 2026
NewCollaboration

Teams, revisions and comments pinned to the plan

bimp is now a shared workspace. Put projects in a team, keep every issue of a drawing as a revision, and discuss the plan where it matters.

  • Teams. Create a team and add colleagues by email. Everyone in it can upload, ask, comment and label revisions; owners manage members and move projects between teams.
  • Revisions. Each reading of a drawing is a numbered revision with an optional label. Compare any two: rooms added, removed or resized, walls added or removed, doors, windows, net and gross area.
  • Pinned comments. Drop a pin on the plan to start a thread; the room under the pin is recorded. Ask bimp in the thread and it answers from the source sheet with your pin marked, the elements around it and the plan tools.
14 November 2026
ImprovedPlans

Plans drawn to drafting convention, and a Dimensioned mode

The 2D plan now reads like a drawing: cut at 1.2 m, walls hatched by type (45° hatch for masonry from 250 mm, cross-hatch for load-bearing walls of 150–250 mm, open partitions), line weights after ISO 128-2 from 0.70 mm down to 0.16 mm, and anything above the cut dashed.

A new Dimensioned mode adds facade, structure and overall dimension strings on every side, room clear sizes and door widths, in millimetres. The drawing sheet is white and bimp now uses a single light theme.

14 November 2026
NewPlans

Room and plan info, door swings, layout suggestions

  • Click a room for net area, length × width, perimeter, ceiling height, volume, doors, windows and glazing. With nothing selected, the plan shows net, wall and gross area and floor level.
  • Doors swing by the usual rules: into rooms rather than corridors, out of cupboards, entrance inward. A door whose leaf hits furniture is flagged.
  • Feng shui checks as traditional guidance, and suggested furniture for empty rooms, drawn dashed and never treated as drawing data.
14 November 2026
Improved3D

Dollhouse 3D, cleaner walls and real furniture

  • Dollhouse is the default 3D view: walls between you and the rooms drop away and come back as you rotate. Cut 1.2 m and Full are one click away.
  • Floors in wood, tile, concrete or decking by room type, a visual convention rather than drawing data.
  • Walls meet with mitered corners and soft contact shadows, after the techniques in neonplan3d (MIT).
  • Fittings use the Kenney Furniture Kit (CC0), scaled to the size read from the drawing.
14 November 2026
FixedSecurity

Reliability, security and accessibility fixes

  • Walls close their corners and pair faces of any thickness; detail scales no longer override the plan scale.
  • A conflict between the stated scale and a dimension now makes the plan a preview.
  • Escape distances are sampled along walls and inside rooms; the answer verifier is stricter.
  • Per-person limits: one drawing read at a time, 200 questions a day. Parsing limits on uploaded files.
  • Rooms reachable by keyboard, chat updates announced to screen readers, the info panel docks beside the plan.
14 November 2026
Improved

New look and a new site

A new identity: the bimp mark, paper and charcoal with an orange marker, set in Jost. The dashboard was redesigned and the website now covers the product, pricing, this changelog and the docs.

13 November 2026
NewChecks

Checks against design standards, escape routes and wall thickness

bimp now checks plans against the Metric Handbook (4th ed.): preferred scales, room minimums, bed sizes, door, corridor and stair widths, garages and daylight. Each flag cites the chapter and table it comes from.

  • Escape: travel distance from the worst point of each room to an exit, drawn on the plan as an overlay.
  • Wall thickness is measured from facing room edges, so net areas stay to the inside of walls.
13 November 2026
NewPlans

Fittings and furniture read from the drawing

WCs, sinks, baths, showers, cookers, beds, sofas, tables, desks and wardrobes are read from the drawing, placed against their walls and assigned to rooms, then drawn in 2D and 3D. Ask "how many WCs?" and bimp counts them.

13 November 2026
NewReading

Drawings read in the background, measured when exact

Uploads are now read by a background pipeline: analyse the file (PDF, scan, image or DXF), read it with an AI model, then measure in code, each step retried on its own.

  • A plan is measured only when its scale is stated exactly and at least 90 % of room corners snap to the drawing's line work. Everything else is labelled a preview.
  • Scale shown as 1:x on screen with a scale bar that is always correct; Print produces an exact 1:x on A4 to A0.
13 November 2026
New

First release: projects, chat and the plan in 2D and 3D

  • Sign in with email and password; your projects and chat history are saved.
  • Upload a floor plan and get a 2D plan and an isometric 3D model, labelled as a preview, with cutaway walls.
  • Ask questions in a chat beside the plan; answers cite the rooms they used.
