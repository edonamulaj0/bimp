How we work

  • Traced, or not said

    Every number bimp shows comes from the drawing or from a calculation it shows you. When it can't find something, it says "not found" instead of guessing.

  • Standards with sources

    Checks cite the chapter and table they come from. They are guidance for the professional, never a verdict on their behalf.

  • Your drawings stay yours

    No advertising, no selling data, no training models on your projects. See the Privacy Policy.

  • Built in the open

    What changes, and when, is in the changelog. Methods and limits are in the docs.
