Where bimp comes from

Junction 2026: The European Tech Renaissance. 48 hours, one question: who moves the center of gravity?

  • Built in 48 hours

    bimp was made at Junction 2026, which calls itself Europe's largest hackathon: 13 to 15 November 2026 at Hype Arena in Espoo, in the Helsinki region. Teams of one to four take an idea from nothing to a working prototype in one weekend. We were three.

  • The theme

    This year's theme is the European Tech Renaissance: a call to question the model we have inherited and build the one that comes next. Copernicus did not find a new star. He refused to accept where everyone had put the centre.

  • The centre we moved

    On a building project the centre has been whoever remembers where things are in the drawing set. Everyone else asks them, or guesses. bimp puts the drawing at the centre: every answer comes from the sheet and points back to the place on it.

  • The dot on the i

    Look at our name. The dot on the i is drawn as a surveyor's target, the mark for a fixed point everything else is measured from. That is our centre of gravity, and it is why every answer in bimp is marked on the drawing.

  • The challenge

    We took the challenge on answering questions about construction drawings accurately and verifiably: about 14,000 real drawings in some 30 languages, with about 120,000 quantity questions. The brief was to build something next year's AI models won't make obsolete.

How we work

  • Traced, or not said

    Every number bimp shows comes from the drawing or from a calculation it shows you. When it can't find something, it says "not found" instead of guessing.

  • Standards with sources

    Checks cite the chapter and table they come from. They are guidance for the professional, never a verdict on their behalf.

  • Your drawings stay yours

    No advertising, no selling data, no training models on your projects. See the Privacy Policy.

  • Built in the open

    How bimp reads a drawing, what it measures and where its limits are is written down in the docs.
