About

A pilot through the drawing set.

bimp is short for BIM pilot: the one who knows the way. It takes you from a question to the exact spot on a drawing or model where the answer lives.

Why

Hours a day, spent looking.

Site engineers, project managers and estimators spend a large part of every day searching drawing sets for one number, one note, one detail. General-purpose AI tools often misread complex technical drawings, and an answer you cannot check is a risk on site.

bimp was started for the Junction challenge on answering questions about construction drawings and building models accurately and verifiably. The brief asked for something next year's AI models won't make obsolete. So bimp keeps what must stay correct in geometry, rules and code. The AI model reads and writes, and a better model makes bimp better.

The name and mark

Drawn like a drawing.

The b in the logo is a door swing in plan. The dot on the i is a survey target. The orange is the colour of a marker on a site drawing. The same language runs through the product: sheets, scales, leaders and annotations.

How it's built

Small, fast and checkable.

  • Cloudflare Workers for the app and API, D1 for projects, R2 for drawings, Workflows and Containers for reading them
  • Python in the container: PyMuPDF for PDFs, OpenCV for scans, ezdxf for CAD
  • An AI model (Gemini) to read labels and outlines, and to write answers that cite their sources
  • Preact and three.js in the browser; a 2D plan in SVG and an isometric model in WebGL
