Reading a drawing

Read once, carefully. Answer from that.

Each file goes through the same steps, and each step is retried on its own. The model reads labels and outlines; code does every measurement.

01Upload

PDF, DXF or image, stored privately. Duplicates are recognised by content.

02Analyse

Vector lines, text and the sheet scale from PDFs; wall strokes from scans; exact units from DXF.

03Read

An AI model names rooms, outlines them, finds doors, windows and fittings, copies dimensions as written.

04Measure

Outlines snapped to the drawing's own lines; scale chosen from the sheet; door swings set by rule.

05Plan

Rooms, walls, openings, fittings and levels, marked measured or preview, ready to ask.

2D

The plan, to scale

Walls meet with mitered corners, doors swing into the rooms they serve, fittings sit against their walls. Every room shows its name, area and dimensions.

  • Type 1:50, 1:100 or any ratio; a scale bar is always correct
  • Print at an exact 1:x on the smallest sheet from A4 to A0
  • Overlay the source drawing to check the reading
3D

An isometric model you can turn

In dollhouse view the walls between you and the rooms drop away, and come back up as you rotate. Cut at 1.2 m like a plan section, or show the closed building.

  • True isometric, no perspective distortion
  • Floors in wood, tile, concrete or decking by room type
  • Hover a room in 2D or 3D and it lights up in both
Info

Rooms and the whole plan, in numbers

Click a room for its net area, length and width, perimeter, ceiling height, volume, doors, windows and glazing. With nothing selected you get the plan: net, walls, gross, floor level.

  • Net to the inside of walls; gross adds the walls
  • Heights read from level marks and ceiling notes
  • "Ask AI for an overview" for a written summary
Layout

Furnish an empty plan

For a bare plan, bimp can place furniture by feng shui rules: bed with a solid wall behind it and the door in view, sofa facing the way in, cooker clear of the sink. Suggestions are dashed and never mixed with what the drawing shows.
