The plan, drawn the way you'd draw it

2D, 3D or both side by side. Hover a room in one and it lights up in the other and in the chat.

Plan and Dimensioned modes

Cut at 1.2 m with walls hatched by type and ISO 128-2 line weights. Dimensioned adds facade, structure and overall strings on every side, room clear sizes and door widths, in millimetres.

Isometric 3D

True isometric, turned in 90° steps. Dollhouse drops the walls in front of you, Cut 1.2 m shows a plan section, Full shows the closed building. Floors by room type are a visual convention, not drawing data.

Rooms in numbers

Net area, length and width, perimeter, ceiling height, volume, doors, windows and glazing ratio. With nothing selected: net, walls, gross and floor level for the whole plan.

Suggested layouts

An empty plan can be furnished by traditional feng shui rules. Suggestions are dashed and never mixed with what the drawing shows.

Scale: type 1:50, 1:100 or any ratio; the scale bar is always correct. On-screen scale is nominal because screens differ; Print is exact, on the smallest sheet from A4 to A0 that fits.

Work on it together

Projects belong to you or to a team. Everyone with access can upload, ask, comment and label revisions.

Revisions

Every reading of a drawing is a revision, numbered from 1. Give each a short label. Compare any two: rooms added, removed or resized, walls added or removed, doors, windows, net and gross area.

Pinned comments

Pin a comment to a point on the plan; bimp records the room under it. Ask bimp in the thread and it answers from the source sheet with your pin marked, the elements around it and the plan tools.

Teams

Create a team, add colleagues by the email they signed up with. Owners add and remove members and move projects in or out; deleting a team returns its projects to their creators.

Checks that name their source

Room sizes, bedroom widths, corridor and stair widths, door widths, wall thickness, garages, daylight and escape distances, from the Metric Handbook (4th ed.). They're shown on the plan and available to the chat. They point at misreadings and problems worth a look; they aren't a compliance verdict.

Measured or preview

A plan is measured only when the drawing states its scale exactly and at least 90 % of room corners land on the drawing's own lines. Everything else is a preview, and the canvas says so.
