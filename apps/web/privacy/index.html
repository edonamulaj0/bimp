Who we are

bimp (Building Information Modeling Pilot) is a hackathon project built at Junction 2026 and run by its three authors, Edona Mulaj, Leart Lama and Arlind Mulliqi ("bimp", "we"). There is no company behind it. We are the controller of the personal data described here. Questions about this policy or your data: message any of us on LinkedIn, linked on the About page.

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • Project content: drawings and files you upload, the plans bimp reads from them, revisions and their labels, comments, pins and chat questions and answers.
  • Team data: team names, members' email addresses and roles.
  • Technical data: a session cookie, and short-lived security records (failed sign-in attempts and the network address they came from) used to stop abuse.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

AI processing of drawings

To read drawings and answer questions, bimp sends the relevant drawing images, extracted plan data and your question to an AI model provider (Google, Gemini API) acting as our processor. We use the paid API terms, under which inputs are not used to train the provider's models. Answers are checked against the plan geometry by bimp's own code before they are shown.

Measurements and design-standard checks are computed by bimp's own code on our infrastructure.

Two optional features send content to a further provider, Cloudinary, and only when you use them. Images uploads a picture of your 2D plan so that a presentation image can be generated from it with Cloudinary's generative AI. Share as image, on a source shown under an answer, uploads the sheet the answer cites and creates a link to the cited part of it. Anyone who has that link can open the image without signing in.

Where it is stored, and who processes it

bimp runs on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 database, R2 file storage, Containers). Drawings and project data are stored there. Our processors are:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, database, file storage and network.
  • Google LLC: AI model processing (Gemini API), as described above.
  • Cloudinary Ltd.: generating presentation images and hosting images you share, only when you use those features.

Some processing may take place outside your country, including in the United States. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on the processors' Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

  • Account and project data: while your account exists. Deleting a project removes its files, plans, revisions, comments and chat from our database and storage, and its generated and shared images from Cloudinary.
  • You can close your account in the app, under Account, Delete account. Your personal projects are deleted with it straight away; projects you created in a team stay with the team. Anything that remains is deleted within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • To avoid reading the same sheet twice, we keep the AI provider's reading of a sheet, and answers that passed our checks, in a cache. Cached entries are not linked to an account or a project and are not removed when a project is deleted.
  • Sessions last 7 days.
  • The demo ("See the demo" on the sign-in page) runs on a throwaway account with no email address or other personal data. It is deleted when you leave it, and otherwise within two days.
  • Security records of failed sign-ins: 24 hours.
  • Backups held by our hosting provider roll over on their own schedule.

Cookies

bimp sets one strictly necessary cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is HTTP-only and not readable by scripts. The app also keeps interface preferences in your browser's local storage (theme, panel sizes, layers, the view you last used, whether you have seen the welcome tour and the first-project guide). They stay on your device and are not removed when you sign out.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Message any of us on LinkedIn, linked on the About page; we answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Team owners control the projects shared with their team: if you are a member, some requests about shared project content may need to go through the team's owner.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; sign-in is rate-limited. Uploaded drawings are served back by bimp only to people with access to the project, sandboxed so they can never run as a web page. Images you choose to share with "Share as image" are public to anyone who has the link.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top, and for material changes we tell signed-in users in the app before they take effect.
