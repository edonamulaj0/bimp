Who we are

bimp (Building Information Modeling Pilot) is operated by [legal entity name], [registered address] ("bimp", "we"). We are the controller of the personal data described here. Questions about this policy or your data: [privacy contact email].

What we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • Project content: drawings and files you upload, the plans bimp reads from them, revisions and their labels, comments, pins and chat questions and answers.
  • Team data: team names, members' email addresses and roles.
  • Technical data: a session cookie, and short-lived security records (failed sign-in attempts and the network address they came from) used to stop abuse.

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell personal data.

AI processing of drawings

To read drawings and answer questions, bimp sends the relevant drawing images, extracted plan data and your question to an AI model provider (Google, Gemini API) acting as our processor. We use the paid API terms, under which inputs are not used to train the provider's models. Answers are checked against the plan geometry by bimp's own code before they are shown.

Measurements, checks, the 2D plan and the 3D model are computed by bimp itself and do not leave our infrastructure.

Where it is stored, and who processes it

bimp runs on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 database, R2 file storage, Containers). Drawings and project data are stored there. Our processors are:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting, database, file storage and network.
  • Google LLC: AI model processing (Gemini API), as described above.

Some processing may take place outside your country, including in the United States. Where the GDPR applies, transfers rely on the processors' Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

How long we keep it

  • Account and project data: while your account exists. Deleting a project removes its files, plans, revisions, comments and chat from our database and storage.
  • When you close your account, we delete its data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.
  • Security records of failed sign-ins: 24 hours.
  • Backups held by our hosting provider roll over on their own schedule.

Cookies

bimp sets one strictly necessary cookie: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It is HTTP-only and not readable by scripts. The app also stores a few interface preferences (panel sizes, the view you last used, whether you have seen the welcome tour) in your browser's local storage; these never leave your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Write to [privacy contact email]; we answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Team owners control the projects shared with their team: if you are a member, some requests about shared project content may need to go through the team's owner.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes; sign-in is rate-limited. Uploaded drawings are served back only to people with access to the project, sandboxed so they can never run as a web page.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top, and for material changes we tell signed-in users in the app before they take effect.
