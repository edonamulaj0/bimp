Questions

Where are my drawings stored, and who can see them?

Uploads go to private storage, are reachable only through your signed-in session, and are visible to you and, if the project is in a team, to that team's members. Files are served back with a sandboxing content policy so a drawing can't run anything in your browser.

To read a drawing, bimp sends its rendered image to an AI model (Google Gemini). Readings are cached by file, so the same drawing isn't sent twice.

Which file formats can I upload?

Vector PDF and DXF (these can be measured exactly), scanned PDFs, and PNG, JPG or WebP images (always a preview). DWG isn't read yet: export it to DXF or PDF. IFC models are next. See Uploading drawings.

How accurate are the answers?

Every number in an answer must come from the plan, your question or a stated assumption, or the answer is marked Unverified; if the drawing doesn't hold the answer, bimp says it wasn't found. A plan is marked measured only when the drawing states its scale exactly and the room outlines match its own line work; everything else is labelled a preview.

We'll publish accuracy by question type once our evaluation has run. We don't quote figures we haven't measured. See Method.

What happens when the pilot ends?

We'll announce pricing on this page before anything changes. Your projects stay yours.

Do my teammates need their own accounts?

Yes. A team owner adds members by the email they signed up with. Members can upload, ask, comment and label revisions in the team's projects; only owners delete projects or move them between teams.
