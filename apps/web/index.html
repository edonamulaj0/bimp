05On the jobFour steps

From the drawing to the order sheet.

01

Drop in the drawing

PDF, DXF or a photo of the sheet. bimp reads it into rooms, walls, openings and fittings, to scale when the drawing states one.

02

Ask, or mark an area

Ask about the whole building, or drag a box over the part you're pricing. Answers come only from what's inside it.

03

Check the working

Areas, lengths, counts and the sum itself are shown, with the rooms they came from. Unverified numbers are marked.

04

When it changes

The next issue becomes the next revision. Compare the two and see which rooms, walls and areas moved before you reorder.

06What it doesToday, in the pilot · Photograph above: Divaris Shirichena

Less arithmetic. More building.

  • Quantities with the sum shown

    Tiles, flooring, skirting, paint, screed and volumes from the measured plan, with waste you set and the calculation written out.

    How answers are checked

  • Drawings in, plans out

    Vector PDF and DXF read to scale; scans and photos as labelled previews. Up to 50 MB a file.

    Plans and dimensions

  • Counts you can point at

    Doors, windows, WCs, sinks and fittings, each one marked on the plan so you can see what was counted.

    Asking

  • Notes pinned to the spot

    Pin a comment where the problem is; the whole crew sees it on the plan, and bimp can answer in the thread.

    Comments and pins

  • Revisions compared

    Rooms added, removed or resized between issues, and the change in net and gross area.

    Teams and revisions

  • Print to scale

    Any 1:x on screen; print at an exact paper scale on the smallest ISO sheet that fits.
