About bimp

Drawings are the record.
bimp makes them answer.

Drop in a plan and bimp reads it into rooms, walls and openings, draws it in 2D and as a model you can walk around, and answers questions about it. Every number traced to the sheet, or "not found" instead of a guess.

The sheet

One drawing, drafted again line by line.

bimp redraws every sheet as a clean plan: walls by type, door swings, fittings, names and areas. Dimensioned on all four sides when you need it, printed to an exact scale when you don't.

What a sheet can do
once bimp has read it.

Five things you do with drawings every day, in one workspace your whole team shares.

  1. (01)

    Ask

    Answers with sources

    Ask — in plain words, in your language.

    • Areas, sizes, volumes, net and gross
    • Counts of doors, windows and fittings, each cited
    • "Not found" instead of a guess
  2. (02)

    Mark up

    New

    Mark up — drag a region over the plan or the model.

    • bimp lists the rooms, openings and fittings inside
    • Answers come from inside the region only
    • Measure between snapped corners
  3. (03)

    Walk the model

    2D and 3D

    Walk the model — orbit, pan and zoom freely.

    • Isometric, top, front, side; perspective or parallel
    • Cut at 1.2 m like a plan section
    • Architectural images from any view
  • (04)

    Compare revisions

    Each new issue is the next revision. Rooms added, removed or resized, and the change in area.

    Revisions
  • (05)

    Check the standards

    Room sizes, widths, daylight and escape distances, each flag citing its Metric Handbook table.

    Checks

From sheet to model.

  1. The sheet is read

    Line work, text and the stated scale become room outlines with exact areas.

  2. Walls rise

    Wall thicknesses from the drawing; openings cut where the doors and windows are.

  3. Heads and levels

    Ceiling height and floor level when the drawing states them, assumed and labelled when it doesn't.

  4. A model to question

    Orbit it, cut it at 1.2 m, mark a region and ask.
