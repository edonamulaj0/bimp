Ask your drawings

Ask in plain words, in your language. Every answer lists the rooms it came from; click one and the plan jumps to it. A number that can't be traced to the plan is marked unverified.

  • Areas, sizes, heights, volumes, net and gross
  • Counts of doors, windows and fittings
  • "Not found in the drawings" instead of a guess
How answers are checked

How big is bedroom 2, and will a 1.60 m bed fit with 70 cm each side?

Bedroom 2 is 11.40 m² net, 3.80 × 3.00 m. A 1.60 m bed with 0.70 m on each side needs 3.00 m, so it fits along the 3.80 m wall.

Revisions you can compare

Upload the new issue of a drawing and it becomes the next revision, not a new file. Label it, then compare any two: rooms added, removed or resized, walls, doors, windows, and net and gross area.

  • Full history, newest first
  • Short labels on every revision
  • Changes listed room by room

  • Escape route drawn on the plan
  • Scale conflicts shown, never hidden
